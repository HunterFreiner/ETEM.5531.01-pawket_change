# 🐾 Pet Care Service Website

## 📋 Overview
- This project is a website for a pet-care service designed to provide customers with an easy and convenient way to learn about our services, view pricing, and book pet care and grooming services.
- The goal of the project is to create a professional, user-friendly website that clearly communicates our services and provides a simple booking experience for customers.

## 🎯 Project Goals
- Create a professional and welcoming online presence for the pet-care business.
- Clearly communicate available pet-care and grooming services.
- Provide transparent and easy-to-understand pricing.
- Allow customers to request pet-care bookings online.
- Allow customers to request grooming services online.
- Communicate the experience and values of the business.
- Establish consistent branding across the website.
- Create a foundation for future marketing and business growth.

## ✨ Features

### 🏠 Home Page
- Business introduction
- Overview of available services
- Clear calls to action for booking services
- Consistent business branding

### 🐶 Pet Care Booking
Customers will be able to:
- Select their desired dates.
- Select pickup and drop-off times.
- Review their booking information.
- Submit a booking request.
Future enhancements may include:
- Pet and owner information
- Special care instructions
- Dietary or medication information
- Additional booking notes
- Booking confirmations

### ✂️ Grooming Services
Customers will be able to select from available grooming services, such as:
- Haircuts
- Nail trimming
- Baths
- Brushing/deshedding
- Ear cleaning
- Other available grooming services

### 👥 About Us
The About Us page will provide information about:
- The business
- Our experience
- Years in business
- Our approach to pet care
- Why customers should choose our services

### 💰 Pricing
The website will provide transparent pricing for:
- Daycare
- Overnight stays
- Grooming services
- Optional add-ons
- The pricing structure will be designed around the business's target audience while avoiding hidden or mandatory fees.

## 🎨 Branding
The project will establish a consistent visual identity, including:
- Business logo
- Brand colors
- Typography
- General design guidelines

## 📈 Marketing Strategy
A marketing strategy spike will be used to research:
- Target customers
- Local competitors
- Social media opportunities
- Local SEO
- Online advertising
- Referral and loyalty opportunities
- Other potential marketing channels
