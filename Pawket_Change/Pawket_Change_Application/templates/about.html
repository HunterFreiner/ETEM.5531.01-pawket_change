About Us

Honest care. Honest pricing. That’s the whole idea.

At Pawket Change, we believe caring for your dog should fit into your everyday life—and your budget. We’re here to make dog care simple, affordable, and personal, with clear pricing and a welcoming approach. We’ve proudly been open for one year, and we’re grateful to every dog and owner who has trusted us along the way.

Care That Starts with Listening

Every dog has their own personality, routine, and needs. We take the time to learn what matters to you and your dog, so you can feel comfortable sharing preferences, asking questions, and planning their care.

Clear Price, Clear Choice

Finding care shouldn’t feel complicated. Our approach is straightforward: explain your options, make costs easy to understand, and help you choose what works for you. No pressure to add extras.

Why Pawket Change

We focus on what matters most: attentive care, open communication, and fair prices. Whether you’re balancing classes, work, or a busy week, our goal is to make caring for your dog a little easier.
